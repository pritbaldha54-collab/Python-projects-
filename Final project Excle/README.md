## 🌟 Project Overview

This project explores retail transaction data to understand sales
performance, customer purchasing behavior, product demand, and regional
revenue. The Excel workbook combines transaction-level data with summary
analysis, pivot tables, a sales dashboard, scenario planning, and a
linear regression output.

The goal is to turn raw sales records into useful insights that support
better business decisions.

## 🎯 Objectives

-   Measure overall revenue, transaction volume, quantity sold, and
    average transaction value.
-   Identify high-value customers and frequently purchased products.
-   Compare revenue across regions and customer segments.
-   Organize sales data by date and month for time-based analysis.
-   Present key findings in a dashboard and supporting analysis sheets.
-   Explore growth scenarios and a linear regression summary.

## 📁 Workbook Structure

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Worksheet                           Purpose
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  **Data**                            Transaction-level records,
                                      including transaction/date,
                                      customer, product, quantity, unit
                                      price, payment method, region,
                                      customer segment, and calculated
                                      fields.

  **Analysis**                        Summary KPIs, customer revenue and
                                      purchase frequency, and product
                                      quantity summaries.

  **Scenario Summary**                Compares current values with low,
                                      medium, high, and very-high growth
                                      scenarios.

  **Linear Regression**               Regression summary output for
                                      exploring relationships in the
                                      data.

  **Pivot Table**                     Aggregated sales totals by product,
                                      region, and customer segment.

  **Dashboard**                       Sales performance dashboard with
                                      visual summaries of the workbook's
                                      key metrics.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🚀 How to Use

1.  Open `Final-project.xlsx` in Microsoft Excel.
2.  Start with the **Dashboard** sheet for a high-level overview.
3.  Review **Analysis** for KPIs, customer performance, and product
    summaries.
4.  Use **Pivot Table** to compare product, region, and customer-segment
    sales.
5.  Inspect **Data** to review the underlying transaction records.
6.  Explore **Scenario Summary** and **Linear Regression** for
    additional analytical perspectives.
7.  If you change source data, refresh relevant pivot tables and verify
    formulas and dashboard outputs.

## ⚠️ Notes & Limitations

-   The results are only as reliable as the data and formulas in the
    workbook.
-   Scenario analysis depends on the assumptions entered in the scenario
    worksheet.
-   Regression results indicate statistical relationships in the
    selected data; they do not by themselves prove causation or
    guarantee future sales.
-   Review formulas, pivot-table refresh status, and dashboard
    references before using the results for formal reporting.
